What's included

Everything a teacher actually needs

Designed for daily use — simple enough to open every morning, detailed enough to track the full year.

☀️

Morning & Lunch Attendance

Mark attendance twice a day. One tap for present, one for absent. Mark all students at once or one by one.

📅

Calendar Navigation

Green dots mark days with recorded attendance. Click any date to review or edit that day's records instantly.

📊

Automatic Reports

Attendance percentage per student, colour-coded. No manual calculation — it updates as you mark.

👩‍🎓

Full Student Profiles

Apaar ID, PAN, date of birth, parent names, address, admission number, admission date — all in one place.

🔒

Secure Login

Password required every visit. Locks after failed attempts. Change your credentials anytime from Settings.

💾

Auto-saves, No Server

Data saves automatically in the browser. Works offline. Completely free on GitHub Pages — forever.
